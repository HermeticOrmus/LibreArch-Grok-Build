# Kintsugi ledger: LibreArch-Grok-Build

Kintsugi mends broken pottery with gold, and the repair is the part people look at. This ledger lists every crack found in the v0 edition, the evidence for it, and the seal v1.0.0 puts on it. A row is `sealed` when this release fixes it (the Seal column names the file that shows it), `hallmarked` once the release installs from GitHub, and `open` until someone seals it.

Grades: **hairline** is copy or cosmetic; **fracture** is wrong behavior with a workaround; **break** means it does not work, loses data, or breaks a safety or privacy promise.

v0 is commit [`0ab961a`](https://github.com/HermeticOrmus/LibreArch-Grok-Build/tree/0ab961add4965753d7c7249846cf8f8c382870d5). The pack is [LibreArch-Claude-Code](https://github.com/HermeticOrmus/LibreArch-Claude-Code), pinned at [`c493984`](https://github.com/HermeticOrmus/LibreArch-Claude-Code/tree/c4939847c5c341a4b92b82fce885dbf5af59ffe3). Repros ran with Grok Build 1.0.44 in a fresh `GROK_HOME`.
